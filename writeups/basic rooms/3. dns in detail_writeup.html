Foundational Training: DNS Architecture & Interrogation

Module Objective: Validated core competencies in external network reconnaissance. Effectively interrogating the Domain Name System (DNS) allows a penetration tester to map an organization's hidden subdomains, extract routing mechanisms, and identify third-party cloud providers through publicly available, highly authorized queries before active scanning even begins.

1. Authoritative Record Interrogation

Successfully executed targeted DNS queries to extract A and AAAA records, mapping domain names to their underlying IPv4 and IPv6 infrastructure. Identified third-party hosting and Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) by analyzing CNAME (Canonical Name) redirection records.

2. Mail Server & Security Policy Analysis

Enumerated email routing infrastructure by extracting and analyzing MX (Mail Exchange) records, prioritizing targets based on numerical routing logic. Interrogated TXT records to identify potential misconfigurations in domain verification and extracted hidden flags used for administrative proof-of-ownership.

3. DNS Resolution Mechanics

Mapped the complete DNS resolution pathway, differentiating the distinct roles of Recursive, Root, TLD, and Authoritative servers. Demonstrated understanding of cache poisoning vulnerability windows by analyzing TTL (Time to Live) metrics.
